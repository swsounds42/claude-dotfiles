> **Watch the temperature split.** Short internal messages usually run clipped and economical. Warm external correspondence — thank-you notes, outreach, relationship email — usually runs hotter: exclamation points, repeated thanks, self-aware asides, softer on logistics. Note both, because applying the clipped setting to a warm note is the most common register error.
